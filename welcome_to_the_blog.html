Introduction

Welcome to the official Amlicense (dot) com blog!

Amlicense is a scooter and moped manufacturer specializing in the design and production of high-quality vehicles. Our range spans everything from rugged, adventure-ready enduro scooters to comfortable, practical models built for everyday commuting. We combine thoughtful design, performance, and reliability to create scooters for every kind of rider.

Our approach is:

  • Simple — Simple yet attractive machines
  • Design — Designed for performance and style
  • In-House — Designed from zero - in house.

Everything is developed and manufactured in-house. We don't rely on off-the-shelf engine kits or pre-made assemblies. Our engines are designed, engineered, and built entirely by our own team, giving us complete control over every detail, from the initial concept to the finished product.

Who Are We

We like to describe ourself in three bullet-points:

  • Team — A team of two.
  • Origin — A Belgian engineering firm.
  • Correct — We do not build for something to look good, we build it to **actually be** good.

The Core Engine would be called HX-01, with the following specifications:

  • 6-speed transmission (Enduro)
  • CVT transmission (Scooter)
  • 2-stroke engine (Enduro)
  • 4-stroke engine (Scooter)
  • 50cc displacement (Both)
  • Liquid-cooled (Enduro)
  • Air-cooled (Scooter)
  • Single-cylinder (Both)
  • Kick-start (Enduro)
  • Electric and kick-start (Scooter)
  • Carburetor (Enduro)
  • Electronic fuel injection (Scooter)
  • CDI ignition (Enduro)
  • ECU-controlled ignition (Scooter)

What do we do?

We do the following:

  • Engineer — Engineer everything in-house.
  • Review — Audit all drawings and schemas.
  • Manufacture —Manufcature all components to be one.

Conclusion

Amlicense is built around a simple idea: create machines that are genuinely well-engineered, rather than simply designed to look good. From the initial concept to the finished component, we believe in understanding, designing, and building what we put our name on. As a small Belgian engineering team, we have the opportunity to approach things differently. We value simplicity, attention to detail, and complete control over our designs. This blog will document that journey, from our engineering process and engine development to the challenges, experiments, and ideas behind our machines. We're just getting started.
